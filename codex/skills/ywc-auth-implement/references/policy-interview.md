# Authentication Policy Interview

Run all nine sections in one focused round. Record each selected-method field as `question`, `response/default`, and `approved | deferred-with-risk`. Do not record credentials.

## 1. Authentication methods

Select email/password, each OAuth provider, passwordless, or another approved method. Mark every unselected method as excluded by approved policy.

## 2. Email/password readiness

Only when selected: configuration, reset, established hashing-library boundary, breach policy, and rate limiting.

## 3. OAuth readiness

For each selected provider: app-registration state, callback/redirect URI, consent-screen state, test identity, and non-secret readiness evidence.

## 4. MFA

Enrollment, recovery, enforcement scope, and fallback policy.

## 5. Sessions and devices

Storage, TTL, rotation, revocation, device visibility, and concurrent-session policy.

## 6. Profile and account lifecycle

Profile fields, verification, deletion re-authentication, and retention.

## 7. Authorization

Shallow RBAC roles, default role, and claim/permission boundary.

## 8. Consent and legal drafts

Consent version, capture, withdrawal, and legal drafts marked `법적 검토 전 임시본`.

## 9. Abuse and recovery

Rate limits, verification controls, recovery flow, alerts, and support escalation.
